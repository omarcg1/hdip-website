What we do

HDIP works with women as leaders, candidates and community representatives — not only as voters. Training covers feminist leadership, political participation, gender equality, human rights, the family protection law, non-violence, first aid and mental health, alongside coalition building and advocacy.

  • Leadership trainingFeminist leadership, political participation, the electoral process and campaign management.
  • Rights and legal awarenessGender equality, human rights, the family protection law and non-violence.
  • Coalition buildingMeetings that identify shared goals and issues across women's groups and communities.
  • Experience sharingDirect meetings with women who have previously stood in elections.
  • Health and wellbeingFirst aid and mental health training delivered within the women's programme.
  • Public platformsConferences that bring women and decision-makers into the same room.

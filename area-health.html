Health & Health Education

Health awareness, first aid and preventive health for women, students and communities.

What we do

Health for All — awareness and health education. HDIP delivered a series of specialised training courses in 2024 for different age and social groups: women, university students and school students. The courses covered health, psychological and preventive topics, building participants' capacities, improving their awareness of health issues and behaviours, and strengthening the idea of health as a basic right.

  • First aid coursesTheoretical and practical training on wounds, bleeding, fractures, burns, choking, fainting and CPR.
  • Women's mental healthBasic concepts of mental health, stress management, coping with anxiety and depression, and psychological support during pregnancy and childbirth.
  • Health awarenessPhysical and psychological change, prevention of risky behaviours and building self-confidence.
  • Disease preventionChronic and infectious disease, maternal health, family planning and nutrition during pregnancy and breastfeeding.
  • Environmental healthAir and water pollution, the effects of chemicals, and improving environmental conditions for public health.
  • Health education workshopsSchool health, personal hygiene, first aid, healthy nutrition, environmental health, psychological first aid and child health.

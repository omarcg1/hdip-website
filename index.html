Since 1989 Health Development Information and Policy Institute

A healthy, educated and empowered Palestinian society.

The Health Development Information and Policy Institute is an independent, non-profit Palestinian organisation. We work through research, health education, educational support, capacity building and advocacy — with women, youth, students and underserved communities across the West Bank, Jerusalem and Gaza.

Who we are

An independent Palestinian institute, founded in 1989

HDIP was established in 1989 by a group of researchers, health practitioners and development experts, in response to the urgent need to improve health care in Palestine.

  • 1989Founded in Ramallah
  • IndependentNon-profit, non-governmental

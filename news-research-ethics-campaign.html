Research Ethics Campaign reaches 200 students across 8 universities

Research, Policy & KnowledgeRight to Education

University youth leadership training · 2024 — © HDIP

A campaign on academic integrity, with a simplified guide to preparing scientific research distributed as a lasting reference.

The Scientific Research Ethics Campaign reached 8 local universities and engaged 200 students (120 female, 80 male).

As part of the campaign, a simplified guide to scientific research preparation was distributed, giving students a sustainable reference tool to promote academic integrity and improve the quality of graduation and research projects.

An accompanying awareness campaign against plagiarism ran across several universities, with practical workshops in Hebron, Bethlehem, Nablus and Ramallah.

Alongside the campaign, 768 students (460 female, 308 male) took part in workshops on scientific research, technology and life skills, delivered across 144 training hours covering hypothesis formulation and results analysis, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity and programming, and communication, time management, critical thinking and teamwork.

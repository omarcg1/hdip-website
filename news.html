News & stories

Single activities and events drawn from HDIP's 2024 reporting.

Closing conference audience · Ramallah, 2024 — © HDIP · Photography & credits

2024

85 first aid courses reach 1,700 students across seven governorates

Theoretical and practical training on responding to emergencies — wounds, bleeding, fractures, burns, choking, fainting and CPR.

 Health 2024

First aid for children injured during incursions — two courses in Jenin

45 women trained in how to treat children wounded during military incursions, using techniques suited to children's psychological and physical needs.

 Health 2024

More than 200 women and men meet decision-makers at the closing conference

The Women Able to Change Society project closed in Ramallah with a conference where women told their own success stories.

 Women's Empowerment 2024

Right to Education reaches students in displacement settings in Gaza

345 activities across eight governorates including Gaza, reaching 8,321 participants through an emergency-adapted plan.

 Education 2024

AI tools, creative competitions and research clubs enter the classroom

Among 2024's key outputs: AI applications supporting language and mathematics learning, creative competitions, and a scientific research club in every library.

 Education 2025

Research Ethics Campaign reaches 200 students across 8 universities

A campaign on academic integrity, with a simplified guide to preparing scientific research distributed as a lasting reference.

 Research 2025

Fourteen displaced women run summer and winter camps for 380 children

A response to displacement in Jenin and Tulkarm that paid displaced women to run children's camps — income for them, a safe space for the children.

 Women's Empowerment 2025

130 scholarships and 181 jobs: the Right to Education Support Project 2

Scholarships covering tuition for 130 students, an intensive remedial programme for 1,100 school students, and 181 employment opportunities created.

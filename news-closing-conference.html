More than 200 women and men meet decision-makers at the closing conference

Women's EmpowermentWomen Able to Change Society

Closing conference, Women Able to Change Society · Ramallah, 2024 — © HDIP

The Women Able to Change Society project closed in Ramallah with a conference where women told their own success stories.

At the close of the Women Able to Change Society project, a closing conference was held in Ramallah where more than 200 women and men met decision-makers.

The conference gave women a platform to raise their issues and experiences, contributing significantly to their empowerment and to strengthening their participation in political and social life.

During the conference women told their own success stories — a source of pride for the project. They demonstrated their ability to share their experience and to invite other women to take part, reflecting the behavioural change and the positive impact of the training and empowerment programmes delivered.

The project ran for five years across Hebron, Jenin, Tulkarm and Ramallah, working with 35 trainees in each governorate and 16 sites per governorate.

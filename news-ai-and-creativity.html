AI tools, creative competitions and research clubs enter the classroom

Education

Participants, Women Able to Change Society · 2024 — © HDIP

Among 2024's key outputs: AI applications supporting language and mathematics learning, creative competitions, and a scientific research club in every library.

A number of artificial intelligence tools and applications were introduced into the educational process during 2024, including applications that support language and mathematics learning. This contributed to more interactive learning methods and encouraged students to use technology to develop their skills.

A series of creative competitions was organised in writing, arts, innovation and technology. These contributed to discovering and encouraging students' talents, fostering creativity and positive competition, and strengthening students' confidence in their abilities.

Study support groups were formed among students to promote cooperation, knowledge sharing and mutual academic assistance. In addition, a scientific research club was established in each library to encourage students to conduct research and inquiry, develop critical thinking and foster interest in science and knowledge.

Topics related to climate change, sustainability and environmental protection were integrated into training sessions and workshops, alongside practical activities such as clean-up campaigns and tree-planting initiatives.

Women Leaders in Local Councils

Towards effective women's political participation in local communities.

Women Leaders in Local Councils training · 2024 — © HDIP · Photography & credits

At a glance

The project was designed to strengthen women's participation in local public life and increase their knowledge and capacity to engage in decision-making. It focused on supporting women not only as voters, but as potential candidates and community representatives, with an emphasis on leadership skills, electoral processes, communication, advocacy and community engagement.

Ongoing · 2024
  • Format3-day intensive

  • FocusCandidacy and local councils

Objectives

  1. Strengthen women's knowledge and confidence regarding participation in electoral processes.
  2. Encourage women to consider candidacy for leadership positions in local councils.
  3. Support the development of women's groups and networks that can advocate for women's rights and community concerns.
  4. Increase awareness of legal and policy issues related to women's protection, including domestic violence.
  5. Address negative social perceptions and stereotypes affecting women's participation in public life.
  6. Promote awareness of civic and political participation among women, particularly in underserved communities.

Activities and outputs

  • Intensive leadership and political participation trainingAn intensive three-day programme comprising 30 training hours, bringing together women from different communities. The training covered women's leadership and political participation, the electoral process, experience sharing among participants, election campaign management, communication and negotiation skills, and community coalition and alliance building.
  • Experience-sharing sessions with women leadersDirect meetings with women who had previously participated in elections. The sessions gave participants the chance to hear first-hand experience, discuss the challenges encountered, exchange practical lessons and strategies, and ask questions about public and political participation.

Results · 2025 / 2024

A 30-hour intensive structured around four components.

The training brought together women activists and women interested in running for office, from different geographic and marginalised areas.

  • Political and knowledge component: the legal and institutional framework, and the mechanics of local elections under applicable Palestinian law.
  • Leadership and communication: tools for influence, leadership, effective communication and building cross-sectoral alliances.
  • Campaign management and planning: developing an electoral platform, managing resources and media messaging.
  • Experience-sharing and networking: direct meetings with former women leaders.
  • A noticeable improvement in participants' political and legal awareness and their capacity to analyse political issues.
  • A number of participants expressed their intention to run for leadership positions in their local councils.

Results reported for 2024.

  • Increased political and civic awareness — greater understanding of electoral processes and the role women can play in local decision-making.
  • Strengthened leadership skills — practical skills in communication, negotiation, campaign planning, teamwork and coalition building.
  • Increased readiness to consider candidacy, through learning the practical requirements and challenges of electoral participation.
  • Increased confidence and peer learning through interaction with other participants and with women who had stood before.
  • Greater awareness of the barriers to women's participation — social perceptions, stereotypes and practical challenges.

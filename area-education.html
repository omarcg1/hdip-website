What we do

HDIP supports students facing learning gaps caused by school closures, interruptions to education, teachers' strikes and conflict. Work covers supplementary teaching in core subjects, life skills, awareness of the importance of education, and support for students in displacement settings in Gaza.

  • Supplementary school coursesEnglish, mathematics, physics, Arabic, computer skills, science and life skills for grades 7 to 11.
  • University coursesCommunication skills, Microsoft Office, Bisan accounting software, scientific research and management.
  • Awareness workshopsCyberbullying and online exploitation, effective communication, research skills, choosing a specialisation, dropout risks, and the value of reading.
  • Career guidanceWorkshops and dialogue sessions with professionals so students can explore academic and career pathways.
  • Study support groupsPeer groups for cooperation and knowledge sharing, plus a scientific research club in each library.
  • Emergency deliveryEducational support for students in displacement settings in Gaza, maintaining learning through severe disruption.
